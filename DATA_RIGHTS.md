# Scientific source data and software redistribution

This repository contains the MIT-licensed GWEH implementation, numerical
configurations, independently sourced evidence references and software
documentation. The full MIT licence is provided in `LICENSE`; the source
snapshots and provider terms are recorded in `provenance/source_register.csv`.

The original Global Wind Power Tracker workbook, Global Wind Atlas rasters,
Natural Earth and Marine Regions GIS inputs, and independent validation
sources are obtained from their respective providers. Their primary assets
are not part of the GWEH software deposit. Their copyright, database and
attribution terms remain those of the original providers.

Separate GWEH-created regional geometry, derived statistics and validation
products are deposited as Dataset 10.5281/zenodo.23555696. These products
are intended for CC BY 4.0 insofar as the author holds redistribution rights;
upstream restrictions may additionally apply to portions of derived content.
The dataset-specific attribution and rights statement is included directly
with that dataset, alongside its `metadata/source_register.csv`.

The independently archived software has reserved version DOI
10.5281/zenodo.23555661. A reserved DOI becomes publicly resolvable once its
corresponding Zenodo record is published.
