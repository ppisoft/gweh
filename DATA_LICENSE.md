# Data reuse licence and rights scope

**Licence for GWEH-created content:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The licence applies to geometries, analysis tables and documentation created within GWEH for which the author holds distribution rights. The scope of the derived product of each source is given in the `derived_product_scope` column of the source register.

Original source datasets (among others GWPT, GWA4, Natural Earth, EEZ and the independent-validation datasets) are not licensed by GWEH. Their own licences and citation requirements apply. GWEH does not redistribute their original rasters, workbooks or observations; details are in `DATA_RIGHTS.md` and `metadata/source_register.csv`.
